# AI-Usage-and-Impact-on-Students-Professionals

An exploratory data analysis project investigating how artificial intelligence tools are used by students and professionals, with a focus on productivity, time savings, user satisfaction, cost-effectiveness, and AI tool preferences.

## Project Overview

Artificial Intelligence (AI) is becoming an important part of education and professional work. AI tools help users perform tasks, save time, and improve productivity, but their effectiveness and cost can vary across tools and users.

This project explores AI usage patterns and their reported impact on productivity and work or study habits. Using Python and its data analysis libraries, I analyzed a dataset containing **3,000 records and 19 columns** to identify patterns, compare AI tools, and understand the relationship between AI usage, productivity, and cost.

This is a personal project developed to practice and improve my data analysis skills.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze AI usage patterns among students and professionals.
- Investigate the relationship between AI usage and productivity.
- Examine how much time users save through AI tools.
- Compare the popularity and performance of different AI tools.
- Evaluate AI tools in terms of monthly cost and cost-effectiveness.
- Explore users' work or study hours and AI usage habits.
- Identify patterns that may help users make more informed decisions about AI tools.

## Analysis Performed

The analysis explored 10 research questions related to AI usage and its impact on students and professionals.

The main areas of investigation included:

1. **AI Usage and Productivity** — Exploring the relationship between AI usage and reported productivity.
2. **Time Saved Through AI** — Examining weekly time savings associated with AI tools.
3. **AI Tool Performance** — Comparing the reported performance of different AI tools.
4. **AI Tool Popularity** — Identifying the tools used by the largest number of respondents.
5. **AI Cost Comparison** — Comparing the monthly costs of different AI tools.
6. **AI Usage During Work or Study** — Investigating how AI usage fits into users' daily routines.
7. **Cost-Effectiveness** — Evaluating AI tools based on their costs and reported benefits.
8. **User Satisfaction** — Exploring users' satisfaction with their AI experiences.
9. **Accuracy and Satisfaction** — Investigating whether perceived AI accuracy aligns with user satisfaction.
10. **Recommendation Behavior** — Exploring factors associated with users' willingness to recommend AI tools.

## Limitations

- The analysis is based on the available dataset and its recorded variables.
- Findings may not generalize to all students, professionals, or AI users.
- Reported relationships between AI usage and productivity do not independently prove that AI causes productivity improvements.
- Cost-effectiveness depends on the cost figures, outcome measures, and assumptions used in the analysis.
- Further statistical testing and additional data could strengthen the conclusions.
